### Project Overview
Decodelab tasks is an Ecommerce Sales tasks that analyzes e-commerce sales to uncover trends in sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and operatioal efficiency using Microsoft Excel.
### Dataset Description
The dataset contails order-level transaction records including: order ID, customer ID, product name, quantity, unite proce, total proce, payment method, order status, items in cart, coupon code, referral source, and shipping address.
### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
  For: - Data cleaning
  - Pivot tables
  - Pivot charts
  - Conditional formatting
  - Statistical funtions like QUARTILE and IF, and IQR-based outlier detection.
### Data Cleaning and Transformation
Checked for missing values, removed duplicates, standardized Text formats, ensured correct data types, flagged outliers using IQR, formatted currency columns, amd trimmed unnecessary spaces.

### Exploratory Data Analysis 
The analysis focused on key questions such as: 
- Which products generated most of the revenue?
- What are the preferred payment methods?
- Which referral sources drive the most customers?
- What percentage of orders are completed versus cancelled?
- Are there abnormal order values or purchase quantities?
### Key Business Insights
Based on my observation:
1. The total amount that was made from the Ecommerce sale was 1264761.96 
2. Chairs, Printers and Laptops were the top selling products with a total of 593359.28
3. The least selling products are Phone, Desk and Monitor. This could be as a result of high price or low product ads 
4. A total number of 1200 persons ordered the company's product
5. Instagram had the highest referral source, followed by emails and google. 
6. Cancelled and pending orders are high. This could be that, after customers checked the site, they later found out that they could get the desired product at a cheaper rate then went back to cancel or the maybe the delivery process or fee is time comsuming.
7. Majority of the customers uses Freeshipping code with a total of 313 orders
8. The total amount that was made in 2023 was 552643.24, in 2024 was 480235.87 and 231882.85 in 2025 respectfully.
9. The average total amount that was made from the Ecommerce sale was 1053.9683
10. The month of June, March and May experienced high sales.
### Recommendation
Based on the analysis, my recommendation would be that there should be:
- Increase inventory for high demand products
- The company should invest more in strong referral channels
- Encourage efficient payment methods
- Investigate cancelled orders to reduce revenue loss and
- Monitor high-value transactions for fraud detection and customer segmantation.

This project demostrates how Excel can be used to clean, analyze and visualize e-commerce sales dara for data driven decision making.
